Safety

Remove an intimate image

If an intimate photo or video of you was shared on Exchange without your consent, including AI-generated or altered images, tell us here. You don't need an account, and you don't need to know who posted it.

What happens next

We remove content covered by a valid request within 48 hours, along with copies we can find, and we act on the account that posted it. We'll email you when it's done or if we need more detail. We never ask you to send the image itself.

You are

Open the profile in the app, scroll to the very bottom and tap the small blue Profile ID button. No account or can't get it? Fill in as much of the rest as you can.

Have an Exchange account? Reporting inside the app is fastest: open the profile, tap the report button and choose the intimate image reason. It attaches the exact profile for us. You can also email legal@exchangesocialapp.com with the subject "NCII / Take It Down." If the image involves anyone under 18, email childsafety@exchangesocialapp.com and report it to the NCMEC CyberTipline. For support and prevention tools, see StopNCII.org and the Cyber Civil Rights Initiative.
