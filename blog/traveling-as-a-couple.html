Traveling as a couple: how to meet people in a new city

September 27, 2026 · Exchange team

Travel is one of the best parts of the lifestyle. A new city means new people, new venues and a break from your routine. But if you wait until you arrive to start looking, you'll often spend half the trip just getting oriented. A little planning goes a long way.

Start before you pack

The best trips usually begin a week or two ahead. That gives you time to chat with people, get a feel for whether you click, and make plans that fit your schedule. Last-minute connections can be fun, but they're harder to line up when you only have a few nights.

On Exchange, Roam lets you appear in your destination's grid before you get there, with a Visiting badge that shows your travel dates. Locals can see that you're coming and reach out, and you can start conversations days ahead.

Know the local scene

Lifestyle culture varies a lot from city to city. Some places have well-known clubs and regular parties. Others are more private, with most socializing happening through personal invitations. Before you go:

  • Check the Events section for parties and meetups during your dates.
  • Ask locals you're chatting with about their favorite venues.
  • Read up on any dress codes, membership requirements or advance registration that clubs require.

Keep safety in mind

You're in an unfamiliar place, so the usual safety habits matter even more. Meet in public first, keep your own transportation options open, and let a friend back home know your plans. Keep your hotel name to yourself until you're comfortable.

It's also worth confirming local laws and customs, especially when traveling internationally. What's normal at home may not be where you're going.

Be clear about your time

People you meet while traveling know you're only in town for a few days. Be upfront about your schedule and what you're hoping for. Clear communication avoids disappointment on both sides, and it makes it easier to plan something everyone enjoys.

Keep the connection going

Some of the best friendships in the community start on trips. If you hit it off with someone, stay in touch. Many couples plan return visits, or invite the people they meet to visit them in turn.

Plan ahead, stay safe, and enjoy exploring somewhere new together.
