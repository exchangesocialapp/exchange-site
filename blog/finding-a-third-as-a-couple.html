How to find a third as a couple: a respectful guide

October 16, 2026 · Exchange team

Plenty of couples, of every orientation, reach a point where they want to meet a third person together. It can be one of the best experiences you share, or a stressful one. The difference usually comes down to how honest you are with each other, and how you treat the person you meet. Here's how to approach it well.

Agree on what you're actually looking for

"Finding a third" means different things to different couples. Before you message anyone, talk through the details together. Are you hoping to meet a woman, a man, a non-binary person, or are you open? Is this about a one-time experience, an ongoing connection, or seeing where it goes? Will you always meet as a couple, or can either of you spend time with them alone? There are no wrong answers, but you both need the same ones.

Talk about the hard parts before they happen

Jealousy and nerves are normal, even for experienced couples. Decide ahead of time how you'll check in with each other, what a signal to slow down looks like, and what happens if one of you wants to stop. Agree that either partner can call a pause at any time, with no blame.

Write a profile a third would trust

Singles tend to be careful, and for good reason. A clear, warm profile makes a big difference. Show both of you in your photos, write in a voice that sounds like both of you, and be specific about what you're looking for and what you're not. On Exchange, your couple profile shows each partner's details at a glance, so people know exactly who they're talking to. Verifying both partners with Exchange+ or Unlimited tells people you're real, and members you've chatted with can vouch for you too.

Treat your third as an equal, not an accessory

The single person you meet is a whole person with their own feelings, boundaries and preferences. Ask what they're looking for, not just whether they fit what you want. Make sure both of you are talking with them, not just one partner. Rules that only exist to protect the couple, without any thought for the third, are a fast way to hurt someone, and people in the community talk.

Go slow and meet in public first

A drink or coffee in a public place is the best first step. It gives all three of you a chance to see if the chemistry is real, with no pressure. If it's not a fit, a kind and clear "thank you, we don't think we're a match" is always better than going quiet.

Consent is for everyone, every time

Consent should be clear, enthusiastic and ongoing for all three people, and anyone can change their mind at any point. Afterward, check in with each other as a couple, and check in with your third as well. A simple message the next day shows respect and builds trust.

Where to start

On Exchange you can browse nearby singles and filter by profile type, orientation, age and more. Sparks are an easy, low-pressure way to say hello. Traveling? Use Roam to appear in your destination before you arrive. And events are a relaxed way to meet people in person first.

Finding the right person can take time. Stay honest, stay kind, and keep talking to each other. That's what makes the experience good for everyone involved.
