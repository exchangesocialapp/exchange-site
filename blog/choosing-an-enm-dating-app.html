Ethical non-monogamy dating apps: what to look for

October 16, 2026 · Exchange team

Ethical non-monogamy, or ENM, covers a lot of ground: polyamory, the lifestyle, couples dating together, singles meeting couples, throuples and more. Most mainstream dating apps were built for one person looking for one other person, which makes them awkward at best for everyone else. If you're choosing an app for ENM, here's what's worth looking for.

Profiles that fit how you actually show up

If you're a couple or a throuple, you shouldn't have to squeeze into a single person's profile. Look for an app where one profile can belong to two or three people, with details for each partner. It saves everyone time and avoids awkward surprises. On Exchange, every profile shows its makeup at a glance, such as M/F, F/F, M/M, M/F/F or M/M/F, including non-binary partners.

Real people, verified

Fake profiles are a problem on every dating app, and they hit ENM communities hard. Look for verification that's actually checked, ideally by a person, not just a photo upload. On Exchange, Exchange+ and Unlimited members can verify with a photo ID and a selfie, reviewed by our team, and members who've talked can vouch for each other.

Every orientation, not an afterthought

A good ENM app welcomes straight, gay, bi, pan and queer members, and lets you search for exactly who you're hoping to meet. Check that orientation and profile type are real filters, not just text in a bio.

Privacy you control

Many people in ENM aren't out to everyone in their life, and that's their choice. Look for an app that lets you hide your distance, keep private photos private, and choose exactly who sees them. On Exchange, hiding your distance is free, private albums are shared only with people you pick, and Unlimited members can browse without appearing on the grid.

Safety tools that work

You should be able to block and report anyone from their profile or a chat, and those reports should be reviewed by real people. An app that takes safety seriously also has clear rules on consent, a strict 18+ policy, and a way to remove intimate images shared without consent.

Built for travel

A lot of ENM connections happen while traveling. Features that let you show up in a city before your trip mean you can start conversations ahead of time instead of scrambling after you land. On Exchange, that's Roam.

Honest about cost

Check what's free and what's paid before you commit. On Exchange, browsing, messaging and a daily allowance of Sparks are free. Paid plans add more Sparks, more filters and extras like seeing who viewed you.

The right app makes it easier to meet people who want what you want, and harder for anyone to waste your time. Whatever you choose, keep consent, honesty and respect at the center of every connection.
