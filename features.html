Features

Everything you need to meet like-minded people.

Exchange is built for couples, singles and throuples in the lifestyle and open relationships. Here's what's inside the app.

📍

Browse nearby

See couples, singles and throuples near you, sorted by distance. Filter by profile type, age and more, or explore any area on the map before you visit.

👫

Profiles made for couples

Couples and throuples share one profile with details for each partner, and every profile shows its makeup at a glance (M/F, M/M, F/F and more), so you always know who you're talking to.

✈️

Roam

Traveling? Set your destination and dates and show up in that city before you arrive. Locals see a "Visiting" badge so you can make plans ahead of time instead of scrambling when you land.

⚡

Right Now

Short posts that disappear after an hour. Say what you're up to tonight and see who else is out and about.

💬

Chat and reactions

Message your connections, react to messages, and share your location only when you're ready to meet. Read receipts and typing indicators are available on Exchange Unlimited.

🔒

Private albums

Keep personal photos in private albums and share them only with the people you choose. You can revoke access at any time.

🎟️

Events

Discover lifestyle parties, meetups and socials near you. Members can submit events, and every listing is reviewed before it goes live.

✓

Verification and trust

Members can verify with a photo ID and selfie for a blue check, reviewed by a real person. Members who have chatted can also vouch for each other.

Free, Exchange+ and Exchange Unlimited

Exchange is free to download and use. Upgrading unlocks more:

Free

Browse nearby, send sparks, chat, private albums, Right Now posts and events.

Exchange+

More sparks and favorites, advanced filters, see everyone who sparked you, see more of who viewed you, video uploads and no ads.

Exchange Unlimited

Everything in Exchange+, plus incognito browsing, hidden distance, read receipts, typing indicators and unlimited sparks.
