🔒 Private albums

Keep personal photos private and choose exactly who sees them, and for how long.

Public photos vs. private albums

Your public profile photos are seen by everyone and can't show nudity. Private albums are for everything else, and only people you share them with can see them.

Create an album

  1. Go to You, then Albums.
  2. Create an album and add photos or videos from your phone.

Share an album in a chat

  1. In a chat, tap 📎, then Send an album.
  2. Pick one or more albums.
  3. Choose how long they can see it: indefinitely, 24 hours, 1 hour, 10 minutes or view once. The shorter options are included with a paid membership.

They see a blurred cover with the album name and how many photos and videos are inside, then tap to open it. You can stop sharing anytime.
