👫 Couples and throuples

Share one login or give each partner their own, and invite partners with a code.

One login or separate logins

Couples and throuples have one shared profile. You decide how you sign in:

  • One shared login: everyone uses the same email and password.
  • Separate logins: each partner has their own account attached to the same profile,.

You can switch anytime in You, then Couple settings (or Throuple settings).

Invite a partner

  1. Open Couple settings. Empty spots show an invite code.
  2. Tap the code to share it.
  3. Your partner downloads Exchange, taps I have an invite code and enters it.

Remove a partner or leave

In Couple settings, tap a partner's tile to remove them. They lose access to the profile right away and a new invite code is made for their spot. If the profile was verified, the badge pauses until the spot is filled and everyone is verified again.
