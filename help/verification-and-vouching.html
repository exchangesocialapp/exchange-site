✓ Verification and vouching

Get the blue check and build trust through members who've met you.

What the blue check means

A blue ✓ next to a name means that profile passed photo and ID verification, reviewed by a real person. For couples and throuples, the check appears once every partner has verified.

Get verified

  1. Go to You and tap Get verified. Verification is included with Exchange+ and Exchange Unlimited.
  2. Take a selfie doing the pose shown, and add a photo of your ID.
  3. If you share one login, add one selfie of everyone together plus each person's ID.
  4. Submit. You'll get the badge once it's approved.

Your ID is never shown on your profile, and verification files are deleted after review.

Vouching

Verified members can vouch for people they've connected with in chat. Profiles with enough vouches show Community trusted. To vouch, open their profile and tap Vouch.
