👋 Getting started

Create your account, set up your profile and learn your way around.

Create your account

  1. Download Exchange from the App Store or Google Play and sign up with your email.
  2. Confirm you're 18 or older. Exchange is for adults only.
  3. Choose your profile type: couple, throuple, single woman or single man. Couples and throuples pick their makeup (for example M/F or F/F). Non-binary options are under Show non-binary options.
  4. If a partner already made your profile, tap I have an invite code and enter the code they sent you.

Set up your profile

Go to You, then Edit profile. Add a display name, an About me, tags, what you're looking for and details for each partner. Profiles with a clear photo and a real bio get far more replies.

Your public profile photos can't show nudity. Every public photo is checked when it's uploaded. Personal photos belong in a private album, which you share only with people you choose. See Private albums.

Your five tabs

  • Browse: the grid of members near you, closest first.
  • Interest: who sparked you and who viewed your profile.
  • Messages: your conversations.
  • Events: parties, meetups and socials near you.
  • You: your profile, settings, albums and membership.

Online or Incognito

At the top of the You tab you'll see Online and Incognito. Online shows you on the grid. Incognito, included with Exchange Unlimited, lets you browse without appearing to anyone.
