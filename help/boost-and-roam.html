🚀 Boost, Roam and Incognito

Get seen first, show up before you travel or browse without being seen.

Boost

A Boost puts you near the top of your local grid for a set time and lets you see who viewed you while it's active. Tap the round Stand out button on Browse (it switches between 🚀 and ✈️) and choose Boost. While it's running, a countdown shows on the button.

Roam

Traveling? Roam lets you appear in another city before you get there, with a Visiting badge and your dates. Tap the Stand out button on Browse, choose Roam, then pick a city and your trip dates. Roam ends on its own when your trip does.

Incognito and hiding your distance

With Exchange Unlimited you can switch to Incognito at the top of the You tab to browse without appearing on the grid. Anyone, on any plan, can hide their distance in Edit profile → Privacy. You stay in the grid in your usual spot, but your distance isn't shown.
