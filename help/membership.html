⭐ Membership and billing

What's included with Free, Exchange+ and Exchange Unlimited, and how to manage it.

Plans

Free

Browse, sparks, chat, private albums, Right Now posts and events.

Exchange+

More sparks and favorites, more filters, everyone who sparked you, more of who viewed you, verification, video uploads and no ads.

Exchange Unlimited

Everything in Exchange+, plus incognito, hidden distance, read receipts, typing indicators and unlimited sparks.

See the Upgrade screen in the app for current limits and prices.

Manage or cancel

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Exchange. You keep your benefits until the end of the period you paid for.

Restore a purchase

Open Upgrade and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
