📍 Browse, sparks and Right Now

Find people nearby, filter the grid, send sparks and see who's interested.

Browse the grid

Browse shows members near you, closest first. A green dot means they're online. Tap a tile to see their full profile, then swipe through their photos.

Use the filter chips at the top to narrow the grid by type, age, online now and more. Tap the search bar to Explore any area on the map, even before you travel.

Favorites

Tap the ☆ on someone's profile to save them. Tap ★ on the Browse screen to see everyone you've saved.

Sparks

A spark is a quick way to say you're interested. Tap the spark button on a profile. If they spark you back, it's a match and you'll both see it on the Interest tab.

Who sparked you and who viewed you

Open the Interest tab. New sparks and views are highlighted in green until you've seen them. When someone sparks or views you while you're browsing, a small note slides in from the right side of the screen.

Free members see some of these, and upgrading shows everyone. See Membership.

Right Now

Right Now is for short posts about what you're up to today. Posts expire on their own. Tap Right Now on Browse to read posts nearby or add your own. Right Now posts are public, so photos there can't be explicit.
