🛡️ Safety, privacy and your account

Block, report, control what people see and delete your account.

Block or report

On someone's profile, tap ⊘. In a chat, tap ⋯. You can report or block from either. Blocked members can't see or message you. Our team reviews reports and aims to act within 24 hours. See your blocked list in You.

Meeting in person

Chat in the app first, meet somewhere public the first time and tell a friend where you'll be. Read our safety tips and first meetup guide.

Delete your account

  1. Cancel any subscription first in your iPhone Settings. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
  2. Go to You, then Delete account, and confirm twice.

This permanently deletes your profile, photos, albums, messages and login.

Contact us

Email hello@exchangesocialapp.com and a real person will reply. For child safety concerns, email childsafety@exchangesocialapp.com.
