Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Download Exchange, sign up with your email, confirm you're 18 or older, and choose your profile type: couple, throuple, single woman or single man. Couples and throuples can share one login or give each partner their own.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You, then Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, albums, messages and login. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that first in your iPhone Settings.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Exchange. You can cancel anytime; you keep your benefits until the end of the current period.

I bought a subscription but I don't see my benefits.

Open the Upgrade tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us with the approximate date of purchase.

What is a Boost?

A Boost is a one-time purchase that places you near the top of your local grid for a set time and lets you see who viewed you while it's active.

What is Roam?

Roam lets you appear in another city's grid before you travel there, with a Visiting badge showing your dates. It ends automatically when your trip does.

What does incognito do?

Incognito, included with Exchange Unlimited, lets you browse without appearing on the grid. You can switch it on or off at the top of the You tab.

How does verification work?

Exchange+ and Unlimited members can verify with a photo ID and selfie. A real person reviews it, and approved members get a blue ✓ next to their name. Your ID is never shown on your profile.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or your chat with them and use the report or block option. Blocked members can't see you or message you. Our team reviews reports and aims to act within 24 hours.

Someone shared an intimate image of me without my consent.

Use our removal request form. You don't need an account. We remove valid requests within 48 hours, along with copies we can find.

Why can't I upload a certain photo to my profile?

Public profile photos can't contain nudity or explicit content. Every public photo is scanned when uploaded. Personal photos can go in a private album instead, which you share only with people you choose.
